Research Infrastructure and Research Publications Data Model (RIRPDM): Description back to ToC

Imports of fabio and cito are resolved via catalog-v001.xml to locally patched copies (ontology/fabio.owl, ontology/cito.owl): upstream published files assert untyped dcterms:created/modified literals on their own ontology IRI, violating the xsd:dateTime range they themselves declare, which makes the raw remote files OWL-inconsistent (owl:Thing SubClassOf owl:Nothing). Only those two literals were retyped; no domain classes/properties/axioms were altered.
